Fanless laptops used to mean slow laptops. That trade-off is disappearing, and the reason is less about raw speed than about heat density.

The efficiency plateau

Each generation of chip does the same work with less power. Less power means less heat, and less heat means you can pass it through the chassis instead of blowing it out the side.

  • No cut means no relief
  • The camera's movement becomes the pacing
  • Mistakes are unrecoverable

The catch is sustained load: burst performance looks great on a spec sheet, but a thin chassis still throttles under a long export.
